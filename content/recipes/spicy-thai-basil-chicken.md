title: Spicy Thai Basil Chicken
---

[Link](https://foodwishes.blogspot.com/2017/04/spicy-thai-basil-chicken-my-pad-krapow.html)

```ingredients
servings: 3
1 | lb | freshly chopped chicken thighs
1/4 | cup | sliced shallots
4 | clove | garlic, minced
2 | tbsp | minced Thai chilies (or Serrano)
1/3 | cup | chicken broth
1 | tbsp | soy sauce, or as needed
1 | tbsp | oyster sauce
2 | tsp | fish sauce
1 | tsp | white sugar
1 | tsp | brown sugar
| | 1 bunch basil leaves, thinly sliced (about a cup once sliced)
```
