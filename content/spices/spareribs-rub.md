# Spareribs Dry Rub

Makes a large batch — plenty of extra to keep on hand.

## Ingredients
```ingredients
servings: 1
1/4 | cup | salt
1/4 | cup | white sugar
1/4 | cup | turbinado (raw) sugar, or brown sugar
1/2 | cup | ancho chili powder
2 | tbsp | freshly ground black pepper
1 | tbsp | ground cumin
1 | tsp | dry mustard
1 | tsp | cayenne pepper
1/2 | tsp | ground chipotle pepper
```

## Steps

1. Combine all ingredients and mix well.
2. Store in an airtight container until needed.
