# Spices for chicken burritos
## Ingredients
```ingredients
servings: 1
1 | tsp | onion powder
1 | tsp | dried oregano
1 | tsp | salt
2 | tsp | dried cumin powder
2 | tsp | paprika
1/4 | tsp | black pepper
3/4 | tsp | cayenne pepper (optional, adjust to taste)
```
